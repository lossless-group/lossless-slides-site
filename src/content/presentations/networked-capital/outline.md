# Networked Capital, a Primer

*The advantages of Open Innovation in Finance*

Words come from existing Hypernova and Water Foundation copy. The source deck was one slide, so the rest is assembled from `hypernova-site/src/content/narratives-md/` (`fund-with-funds`, `why-us-now`, `venture-pounce`, `courage-to-commit`) and `hypernova-site/src/pages/beliefs/funds-as-instruments.astro`.

---

## Key Pillars

- **Open Innovation** → Leveraging external networks to drive innovation and collaboration.
- **Networked Capital** → Building a network of loosely involved parties to support innovation and collaboration.

---

## The belief

More value is created by the exchange of ideas and knowledge across different organizations, sectors, industries, and even countries, than by small teams of elite professionals working in relative isolation.

---

## A very siloed reality

Impact, Transformation, and Returns are hamstrung by a very siloed reality.

Fund managers only manage whatever kind of instrument or vehicle that they manage, they almost never have more than one fund structure instrument. Why? It's the way the industry grew up. It has always been. No other reason.

We believe funds are instruments of change. And we need a symphony of instruments to create the change we need. No single instrument can play every note.

---

## It's not a normal distribution, it's a power law distribution

For nearly two decades, data from organizations like Cambridge Associates and Pitchbook have shown that Venture Capital can dramatically outperform the market.

Allocating to venture broadly speaking will not outperform the market. Allocating to specific funds that generate those power-law, outsized returns will outperform the market.

The goal should not be to "average out" the returns of individual investments, but to "play to the best."

---

## The "Fund of Funds" model has conundrums

- **Plays to Averages.** Venture is a business of taking bold bets on potential outliers, and outliers are what drives returns. A fund of funds tends to be a bet on "the average."
- **Fees on Fees.** (2% + 20%) + (1% + 10%) = 3% fees & 30% carry. Fees on Fees, Carry on Carry take a huge chunk off a decent return.

---

## Made for Hypernova Capital: a Fund-with-Funds angle on Venture Capital

- Moderate LP Commitments to the next generation of great fund managers.
- Co-Investments and Direct Investments into high-potential, early stage companies.

**Curate & Filter.** We handpick alpha-generating fund managers.
**Build Foresight Engines.** We unite Hypernova's investors with portfolio funds to foster collaboration.
**Structure Aligned Opportunities.** We don't just invest in funds, we double down on their best performers.

---

## Outside of press releases, media, and databases

Constant access to the latest market and technology development comes through deep, interpersonal relationships where information flows outside of press releases, media, and databases.

In private markets, all that matters is being "in the deal."
