## The Problem with Consensus

Most investors follow the same playbook.

- Same deal flow channels
- Same evaluation criteria
- Same herd mentality on hot sectors
- Same FOMO-driven decisions

**Result:** Compressed returns as everyone chases the same opportunities.

---

## Codified Systems for Repeatable Judgement

The ability to anticipate performance at the earliest stages *looks like intuition and luck*, but it's forged from creating a **well-sharpened, experience and information rich theory** on market evolution and company building.

---

## The 12Ps Framework: Five Questions That Matter

- **Origins** → Why would anyone care?
- **Opening** → How big is this?
- **Organization** → Who is building it?
- **Offering** → What exactly is it?
- **Opportunity** → Is it worth it?

---

## Origins: Why Would Anyone Care?

- **Persona** → Who is the target customer?
- **Pain** → What problem do they have?
- **Proposition** → What transformation are you promising?

*Hair-on-fire problems beat nice-to-haves every time*

---

## Opening: How Big Is This Opportunity?

- **Problem** → How many customers exist?
- **Possibility** → TAM, SAM, SOM analysis
- **Positioning** → Blue Ocean vs. Red Ocean

*We look for macro trends amplifying the problem and pointing to this unique opportunity.*

---

## Organization: Who Is Building This?

- **People** → Founder-market fit
- **Process** → Growth engine and best practices
- **Product** → Does it deliver on the promise?

*The right team with the wrong idea can pivot. The wrong team cannot.*

---

## Offering: What Exactly Is It?

How coherently Proposition + Positioning + Product line up.

## Opportunity: Is This Worth Our Capital?

- **Potential** → Projected growth trajectory
- **Progress** → Indisputable, undeniable traction
- **Plan** → Use of proceeds and milestones

*Hard numbers over hopeful narratives*
